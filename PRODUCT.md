# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and growth-minded entrepreneurs across two segments:

1. **Pakistani SMBs and e-commerce brands** — Lahore-based retailers, Shopify store owners, and service businesses looking for a local agency that understands the Pakistani market, speaks to their specific constraints, and delivers measurable returns.
2. **International clients** — Businesses outside Pakistan (including diaspora-owned brands and overseas operators) seeking competitive agency rates with serious execution quality.

When they land on the site they are evaluating: can this team actually deliver results, or is this just another agency with a polished facade? They are skeptical, short on time, and have been burned before.

## Product Purpose

Think Hawks is a full-service digital marketing agency based in Lahore, Pakistan. The site exists to convert skeptical business owners into inquiry leads. Success means a visitor reads the site and thinks "this is the agency I've been looking for" — then submits the contact form or starts a chat.

Services: SEO, social media marketing, content, web development, paid ads (Meta/Google/TikTok), e-commerce (Shopify), branding, and an academy for aspiring digital marketers.

## Brand Personality

Bold, results-driven, authoritative.

The voice is confident without being arrogant. Think Hawks positions itself as the agency that actually delivers, not the one that promises the world. Copy is direct, specific, and grounded in outcomes. No motivational filler, no empty superlatives.

## Anti-references

- **Generic "SaaS cream" agencies**: the warm-beige, eyebrow-on-every-section, soft-rounded-card aesthetic that AI-generated agency sites default to in 2026. Avoid the warm-neutral body background, the identical card grids, the "What We Do / How We Work / Why Us" eyebrow cadence.
- **Corporate/enterprise stiff**: IBM-blue palettes, stock-photo-heavy layouts, overly formal tone. No personality, feels like a procurement vendor rather than a growth partner.
- Avoid hero-metric grids (big number + small label + gradient accent) as the primary trust signal.
- Avoid gradient text as emphasis.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, earn the trust** — Credibility comes from specifics: named services, real metrics, actual team members. Every claim should have evidence nearby.
2. **Authority through restraint** — A confident agency doesn't need to shout. Dark, considered surfaces, deliberate typography, and controlled use of the brand green signal expertise better than visual noise.
3. **Conversion over decoration** — Every section should move a visitor one step closer to making contact. Ornament that doesn't serve that journey gets cut.
4. **Local knowledge, global standard** — The design should feel at home for a Lahore business owner and credible to an international client. Neither provincial nor generically global.
5. **Specificity beats adjectives** — In copy and in design, concrete over vague. "Ranked 200 keywords in 90 days" beats "we deliver results." A sharp typographic grid beats a decorative shape collage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA
- Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against all backgrounds
- All form fields must have associated labels with proper `htmlFor` / `id` pairing
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — animations must have instant-transition fallbacks
- Bilingual audience (English primary, Urdu-speaking users) — keep sentence structure simple and unambiguous
